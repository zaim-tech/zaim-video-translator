# Zaim Video Translator

A project designed to make video content more accessible, multilingual, and globally shareable.

Whether you're creating educational content, product demos, tutorials, or storytelling videos, Zaim Video Translator helps break language barriers so more people can understand and enjoy your message.

## Why this project exists

Video is one of the most powerful ways to communicate, but language can limit who can connect with it. This project aims to make content easier to translate, easier to understand, and easier to share [...]

## What it does

- Translates spoken content from video into another language
- Helps generate subtitle-friendly output for broader accessibility
- Makes multilingual content easier to produce and share
- Supports creators and teams reaching international audiences

## Why it’s interesting

This is more than a translation tool — it’s a bridge between audiences. A single video can become a global story when language stops being a barrier.

## Project goal

To help turn great video content into accessible content for everyone, no matter where they are or what language they speak.

## Getting started

1. Add your video or audio source
2. Choose the target language
3. Run the translation workflow
4. Review the output and share it with your audience
